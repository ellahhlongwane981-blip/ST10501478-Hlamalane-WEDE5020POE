About Ellinah's Stitch Studio 🌸

Creativity, passion and handmade beauty in every stitch.

Our Story

Ellinah's Stitch Studio is a small business founded in 2024 by Ellinah. It started as a simple hobby in knitting and crocheting and has grown into a business where beautiful handmade items are created.

The studio also teaches and empowers young people with skills so that they can use their talents to earn an income and become more independent.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create beautiful handmade knitted and crocheted items while teaching and empowering young people to develop useful skills and build their independence.

Our Vision

Our vision is to help young people turn their talents into sustainable livelihoods and proudly make a living with their own hands.

Our Target Audience

Our target audience includes people who love unique handmade knitted and crocheted items, as well as young people interested in learning knitting and starting their own small craft businesses.
