About Ellinah's Stitch Studio

Ellinah's stitch studio is a small business founded in 2024,
by Ellinah. It started as a simple hobby in knitting and
crocheting has grown into a business where beatiful
handmade items are created.

the studio also teaches and empowers young people skills
so that they can use their talents to create their own
income and become more independent.

Our Mission

our missin is to create beautiful handmade knitted and
crocheted items, and to teach and empower young people
to develop and build their own independence.

Our VIsion

our vision is a small business where young people turn their talents
into sustainable livelihoods, proudly making a living
making a living with their own hands.

Our Target Audience

our target audience are people who love unique, handmade
knitted crocheteditems, and young people interested in
learning knitting to start their small own small craft business
