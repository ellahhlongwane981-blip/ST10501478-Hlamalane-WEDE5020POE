About Our Training

Our training is designed for beginners and young people who want to learn knitting and crocheting. We help learners develop creative skills that they can use to make beautiful handmade products.

What You Will Learn

  • Basic knitting techniques.
  • Introduction to crocheting.
  • How to create handmade products.
  • How to improve your creative skills.
  • How to use your skills to earn an income.
